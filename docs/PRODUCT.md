# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One user: the owner, a solo builder of a systematic futures operation (NQ first). He works on a Mac laptop, often for hours,
switching between the Lab (strategy tester), the Desk (live trading) and Claude sessions. He is not a statistician: he reads
results as stop and target in RR and points ("1:2, 40-pt stop"), how often the full target hits against the win rate needed,
dollars, trades a week, and per year then combined. He picks by number ("box #14"), never by clicking a chart canvas.

Current surface in design: **Missions**, where he gives a team of Claude agents one goal ("a 9:30 straddle that passes an
Apex eval 50 % of the time") and watches them work: the team chat first, then progress, the best version, the heat map,
everything tested, and whether it is real or luck. He can text the team's lead from the page.

## Product Purpose

Homebase builds, tests and runs futures strategies honestly. Success is a strategy he can trust: tested the house way (the
3:1 block walk-forward, hm40 heat maps), judged on the average box not the best one, and shown in plain words. A screen
succeeds when he can answer "what is happening, is it real, what do I do" in one look.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, honest. Plain minimal words, numbers over adjectives, the answer first. (Personality words are Claude's call,
2026-11-13, from his standing feedback; not asked directly.)

## Anti-references

- The 2026-10-27 Desk redesign mockups (`docs/design/phase0/`): he rejected them, "i like the current design more".
- Generic SaaS dashboards: hero metric tiles, identical card grids, gradient accents.
- Statistics jargon on screen: t-stats, R decimals, "cells", ratios.
- Click-to-mark chart canvases (they did not work for him).

## Design Principles

1. Keep the current Homebase look: silver and white, Apple-style, glass only for floating panels.
2. Say it the owner's way: RR and points, full-target % against the % needed, dollars, trades a week, per year then combined.
3. Honesty is visible: the count of tries, the luck bar, sealed test months and failed checks are shown, never hidden.
4. Number everything he might pick (boxes #1-#40, versions v01-v23) so he can answer in chat.
5. The live work comes first; results are one tab away, never a modal away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (body text 4.5:1), every animation has a reduced-motion fallback, color never carries meaning alone
(green/red cells also show the number). (Claude's call, 2026-11-13.)
